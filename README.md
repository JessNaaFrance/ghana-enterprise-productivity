# Women-Led Agro-Processing Enterprises in Northern Ghana

This repository contains the reproducible code used for my Harvard Kennedy School Second Year Policy Analysis (SYPA), which examined productivity constraints among women-led agro-processing enterprises in Northern Ghana.

The project combines nationally representative household survey data from the Ghana Annual Household Income and Expenditure Survey (AHIES) with qualitative field interviews conducted in peri-urban Tamale.

Because the AHIES microdata are confidential and cannot be publicly shared, this repository includes the complete analytical workflow but does not include the raw datasets.

---

## Research Question

Why do women-led agro-processing enterprises remain persistently low-productivity despite years of policy support?

---

## Methods

- Data cleaning and harmonization across multiple survey waves
- Construction of analytical variables
- Descriptive statistics
- Pooled OLS estimation
- Robustness checks
- Reproducible workflow developed in Stata

---

## Repository Structure
